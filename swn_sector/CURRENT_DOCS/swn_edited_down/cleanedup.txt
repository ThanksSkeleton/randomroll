Skills: Skill checks use 2d6+(Rank-1)+Mod
Attacks: Attacks use d20+Attack Bonus+Rank+Mod
Saves: Saves are 1d20 roll-over. 15-Mod Best of (Str,Con) (Dex,Int) (Wis,Cha)

Mods are "flatter"
3: -2
4–7: -1
8–13: +0
14–17: +1
18: +2

Snap Attack: During combat, you can attack out of initiative order. Take a -4 penalty to attack and lose your actual turn.

Expert: Once per scene, you can reroll a failed skill check, taking the new roll if it’s better.
Warrior: +1 Attack Bonus and +2 hp. Once per scene, as a reaction, you can either negate an attack against you OR make one of your missed attacks hit.
Psychic: You have two psychic powers and a pool of 2 Effort. 

Psychic powers

Psychic Powers: Psychic Powers require a Main action and unaided line of sight.
Psychic Powers are undetectable except their explicit manifestations.
<i>As a Psychic, you have undergone extensive training. Psychics are integrated in modern socieites.</i> 
Effort: Effort is “Committed” to use a psychic power. The power description tells you how long it remains Committed.

Biopsionics: Psychic Succor
Commit Effort for the day to automatically stabilize a touched mortally-wounded target. You may use this power for the rest of the scene as well.    
Metapsionics: Psychic Refinement
Passive: You can always hear and see the source and destination of psychic powers, which are normally invisible. +2 to saving throws against all psychic powers.
Precognition: Oracle
Commit Effort for the day to see 1 minute into one of your personal futures. You may "test drive" during this time: for example, you can see what happens immediately when you press a suspicious button. Terms and conditions apply.
Telekinesis: Telekinetic Manipulation  
Commit Effort for the scene to exert force against inanimate objects within your unaided sight. The force is roughly equal to the strength of a normal person. You cannot use this power as a weapon (directly).  
Telepathy: Telepathic Contact
Commit Effort for the day to monitor an intelligent biological creature's emotions for the rest of the scene. Strong emotions may evoke a mental image as well. No save, and the creature is unaware.
Teleportation: Personal Apportation
Commit Effort for the day to teleport yourself and everything you are carrying 10m away as a Move Action. This location must be either currently visible or a place you have seen before. 

Foci

Specialist: Gain a rank in $X. For this skill, roll 3d6 drop 1 lowest instead of the normal 2d6.
Close Combatant: Gain a rank in Shoot. You can use pistol-sized ranged weapons in melee without penalty. You ignore shock damage.
Assassin: Gain a rank in Sneak. You can automatically hide a pistol or knife on your person undetectably. You can draw it from hiding and make a suprise attack, which cannot miss in melee range.
Die Hard: +2 hp. You automatically stabilize.
Shocking Assault: Gain a rank in Stab. Your Shock damage always hits.

Shock: These melee weapons deal the indicated damage + Str Mod on a miss to opponents that have the listed AC or below.
Hidden Armor: Indistinguishable from ordinary clothing.
Street Illegal: On most worlds, this object is illegal, controlled, or attracts unwanted attention. 

Burst Mode: Fire in 3 round bursts for +2 to hit, +2 to damage.
